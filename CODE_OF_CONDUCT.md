# Code of Conduct

NorthCinder's community should be respectful, specific, and safe. Disagreement about evidence, design,
and risk is welcome. Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, deliberate humiliation, and
publication of private information are not.

Give technical criticism with enough context to act on it. Do not pressure contributors to disclose
credentials, identity, account access, private messages, or purchasing history. Do not use project
channels for unsolicited promotion or coordinated contact with third parties.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community. Use GitHub's reporting controls for abuse. If a maintainer needs private
context, open a minimal public issue asking for a private contact route without including the
sensitive details.

Security reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md), not a public conduct discussion.
